This is the new home for Fairport Track & Field. Here's what you'll find:

- **[Schedule & results](/schedule/)**: every meet for the 2026–27 indoor season, with a link to the full Yen Timing results once each meet is posted.
- **[School records](/records/)** and **[all-time top 10 lists](/top-10/)** for every indoor event, boys and girls. When a Raider runs, jumps or throws their way onto a list, it will be updated here.
- **[Varsity standards](/standards/)**: the marks to aim for this winter.
- **[Championship history](/history/)**: how Fairport has done at divisionals, counties, sectionals and states.

Our first meet is Saturday, December 5 at Nazareth. See you there.
